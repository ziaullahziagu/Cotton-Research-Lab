ABOUT THE LABORATORY

Research grounded in
field realities.

Cotton Research Lab connects plant breeding and genetics with the practical challenges facing cotton production.

Our work explores useful genetic variation, crop architecture, earliness, stress tolerance and management approaches that may strengthen cotton performance in changing environments. Field trials and careful data analysis help us evaluate promising material and generate evidence for future research and practice.

We value rigorous science, student development, open exchange of knowledge and partnerships across research institutions, growers and agricultural stakeholders.

RESEARCH PROGRAMME

Our research themes

Our themes bring together genetic improvement and agronomic insight to support productive, resilient and sustainable cotton systems.

THEME 01

Cotton breeding & genetics

Genetic diversity, selection strategies, trait association and development of improved cotton germplasm and varieties.

Research directions ↗
THEME 02

Climate-resilient cotton

Evaluation of cotton responses to heat, drought, salinity and other environmental stresses affecting crop performance.

Research directions ↗
THEME 03

Crop architecture & management

Plant height, branching, earliness, planting density and agronomic practices for efficient cotton–wheat systems.

Research directions ↗
THEME 04

Phenotyping & quantitative analysis

Field-based trait measurement, multi-environment testing, statistical analysis and evidence-informed selection.
